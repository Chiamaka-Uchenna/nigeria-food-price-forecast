The 2023 to 2024 price shock

In mid 2023 Nigeria removed its petrol subsidy and floated the naira. Staple prices in these markets nearly tripled in fifteen months.

Does machine learning beat "no change"?

A gradient boosted model was tested against two simple rules on forecasts it had never seen. It did not win, so the outlook above uses the simple rule. Lower error is better.

Method and limits

  • Data: World Food Programme market monitoring, published on the Humanitarian Data Exchange. Retail prices in Borno and Yobe from January 2017, median across markets each month.
  • Three forecasting methods were compared in a rolling backtest over 36 months, refitted every six months with only past data. The gradient boosted model did not beat the no-change forecast, so it is not deployed.
  • The outlook is the latest price with an empirical 80 percent band for each food category and horizon. Out of sample, the bands contained 82 to 85 percent of actual prices.
  • This covers markets WFP monitors in two states. It is not a national picture, and it cannot anticipate policy shocks.
